One Platform, Three Portals

A lightweight, fully modular dashboard interface catering specifically to hikers, guide guides, and system administrators.

Trekkers (Users)

Explore trails by difficulty or location, view scheduled treks, book multiple tickets, and manage booking history with easy cancellations.

Guides (Staff)

Register to lead hikes (requires Admin approval), view assigned trips, schedule/update slots, and monitor registered participants lists.

Administrators

Perform complete CRUD actions on Trekking Routes, approve/disapprove new Staff registrations, and review system-wide booking statistics.
